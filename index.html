ProPeloton Manager — Online Cycling Management Game

ProPeloton Manager is an online cycling manager game played in a web browser. Create a professional cycling club, build your squad and guide riders through a full season of racing. Rather than controlling a bicycle directly, you manage the choices that shape your team's results.

Build your cycling team and develop riders

Choose riders with different strengths, including sprinters, climbers and time-trial specialists. Manage training, form, fatigue and recovery while building a balanced team. Use transfers, staff, equipment and club finances to plan long-term progress.

Plan races, stage tactics and the season

Review the cycling race calendar and apply for suitable competitions. Prepare your squad for different race profiles, assign riders their roles and set tactics for individual stages. Check race results, rankings and sponsorship goals as your season develops.

Explore the game before creating your club

ProPeloton Manager is a persistent browser-based cycling management simulation for players who enjoy strategy and long-term team development. See screenshots and explanations of the real interface in the public demo, or read the beginner guide and detailed game manual.
